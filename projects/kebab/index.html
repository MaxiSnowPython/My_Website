02 ABOUT US

MEAT.
FIRE.
FLATBREAD.

Seven years we've been spinning marinated meat over live coals and carving it straight into warm, freshly baked flatbread. No pre-packed ingredients, no yesterday's prep.

Head chef Emre came from Istanbul with his grandmother's recipe — and agreed to share it on one condition: the marinade is mixed every evening, the meat is threaded every morning.
